# *The Archaeology of an ISP: How an Internet Provider Really Worked in 1995*

Article by **Marco Iannacone**, published by **Codemotion Magazine** in October 2026 together with the public **Internet Force 1995–1996** repository, which preserves the materials, configurations and infrastructure reconstruction described in the article.

The article follows the recovery of the archive, the context of commercial Internet access in 1995, the design of Internet Force, its POPs, dial-up connectivity, Unix systems, Cisco equipment and FireWall-1, the Welcome Kit, day-to-day ISP work and the traces that survived after the company closed.

## Relationship with the repository

The article and repository were published as parts of the same historical reconstruction project. The article describes the infrastructure, people, processes and recovery of the material; the repository preserves and organises the documents, configurations, maps, software and other surviving material that make Internet Force inspectable in detail.
