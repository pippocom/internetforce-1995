# Infosfera / Bergamo

> **Internet Force 1995–1996 Historical Archive**
> Historical reconstruction and technical documentation based on original Internet Force materials preserved by Marco Iannacone.
> Author and archive curator: Marco Iannacone · https://pippo.com
> License: [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE)

**Infosfera**, based in Bergamo, is one of the provider environments that grew
out of the former POPs, for which Marco Iannacone continued to work as a
consultant after Internet Force's closure. It is not one of Internet Force's
initial POPs.

## Content

The material in this area documents:

- a Cisco 2511 configuration (`CISCO/2511BG.TXT`);
- the Linux system `sax.infosferas.it`, whose `/etc` tree is preserved (`etc/`):
  `named.boot`, `hosts`, `inetd.conf`, `passwd`, `group`, `fstab`, `exports`,
  `xtacacsd-conf` and more;
- a system note, `sysinf.doc`, with the process snapshot taken on the machine:
  `named`, `xtacacsd`, `sendmail`, `inetd`, `crond` and `syslogd` are active.

In the recovered material XTACACS authentication is active on the **Linux
server**. See
[The closure of Internet Force and the later evolutions](../../docs/17-wind-down-and-migrations.en.md).

## Provenance

These files come from the configurations preserved from the Infosfera/Bergamo
environment, after Internet Force's closure.
