              ---------- canalemoda.source --------

@  IN   SOA     canalemoda.com dnsmaster.internetforce.com. (
                1996041801       ; Serial
                10800            ; Refresh 3 hours
                3600             ; Retry 1 hour
                604800           ; Expire after a week
                86400 )  ; Minimum ttl 1 day
                                NS      harley.ios.com.
                                NS      users.internetforce.com 

>E www                  A       206.20.95.20
localhost               A       127.0.0.1

        ------- EOF canalemoda.source ----------


dnsmaster.internetforce.com is where is going its mail..

NS harley.ios.com is saying that the queries for
that domain should come to harley.ios.com.
The other one is to say that should come to us too.
