# `oracolo` — Windows NT + Oracle server

> **Internet Force 1995–1996 Historical Archive**

`oracolo` was a separate machine on the **office LAN**, with address
`206.20.95.142`, based on **Windows NT + Oracle**. It appeared in **1996**.

## Origin of the project

`oracolo` was **not part of the original Unix core** of the ISP. It arose from a
consultancy project requested by a client. Internet Force asked **Marco
Iannacone** to take it on as an **additional activity** beyond his normal role as
system/network administrator.

Marco had **no prior operational experience** with Windows NT and Oracle: he
studied both technologies on his own in about **7–10 days** and delivered the
project. The work also developed skills that Internet Force could later offer in
consultancy. Marco personally named the machine `oracolo`.

## Role in the LAN

`oracolo` was one of the hosts of the office segment; it is listed among the
attached systems in [The office LAN](../office-lan/README.en.md).
