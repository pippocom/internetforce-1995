This directory preserves the article **“DEFCON V — Gli hacker si incontrano”**,
written by Marco Iannacone after attending DEFCON V in Las Vegas in July 1997,
together with the images that accompany it. The content dates from July-August
1997 and is therefore later than Internet Force's operational phase.

## What this is

The text describes DEFCON as a meeting place for hackers, system administrators,
security researchers, journalists and public-sector representatives. Topics
include social engineering, operating-system security, hacker culture, and DEFCON
activities such as Capture the Flag, Hacker Jeopardy, the TCP/IP Drinking Game,
Spot the Fed and the Quake competition.

The article mentions, among others, Ira Winkler, Richard Thieme, Mudge and L0pht
Heavy Industries.

Its author biography describes Marco Iannacone as a UNIX/networking/Internet
systems consultant, System Administrator and Internet-services manager at Dun &
Bradstreet Kosmos, and states that he frequently contributed to `Inter.net`
magazine.

## DEFCON V in 1997

DEF CON V took place in 1997, when DEF CON was still a very young event. The
atmosphere was much smaller and more informal than at later editions: hackers and
security researchers, some of whom would later become well-known figures, shared
the same spaces directly and without the divisions that came later. Marco
Iannacone recalls being able to have a beer and chat easily with several of the
people mentioned in the article.

## Why this material is in the Internet Force archive

The material is **post-Internet Force** and should not be presented as operational
documentation of the ISP. It is preserved here because it was part of the backup
of workstation `marco` and documents continuity in the administrator's technical
path after Internet Force, particularly toward information security.
