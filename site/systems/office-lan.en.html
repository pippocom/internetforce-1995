Context

The office (intf-office, 206.20.95.128/25) was a mixed Macintosh and Unix/Linux working environment behind the firewall’s office interface: editorial and DTP on Macs, the sales area, web authoring and technical administration on Linux. The office network was the protected perimeter from which the firewall GUI and the servers were administered (FireWall-1 rules 10, 11 and 14).

The office’s technical workstation is described on the marco page (Linux, 206.20.95.140).

The image above is documentary: the individual workstations and people are not interactive and have no dedicated pages.
