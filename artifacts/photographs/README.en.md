## Internet Force office (1995)

[`internetforce-office-1995.jpg`](internetforce-office-1995.jpg) is a historical
photograph of the Internet Force office: it shows the rack, Sun servers and
the environment in which the infrastructure was built and managed.

The photograph also shows a young Marco Iannacone, with a few more hairs at the
time.
