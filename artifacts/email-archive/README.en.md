# Historical email archive

> **Internet Force 1995–1996 Historical Archive**
> Historical reconstruction and technical documentation based on original Internet Force materials preserved by Marco Iannacone.
> Author and archive curator: Marco Iannacone · https://pippo.com
> License: [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE)

This directory collects some surviving messages and mailboxes from Internet Force's
activity and the technical work of the period: technical and administrative
correspondence, feedback on guides and documentation, and other exchanges linked
to the ISP and to the work of the time.

The mailboxes are copies of the original mail files, preserved as recovered and
not modified.

## Contents

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`technical/`](technical/) | technical and administrative correspondence: domain registration and management, exchanges with registrars and postmasters (`DOMAIN.mailbox`, `DOMAIN-IDT.mailbox`, `GARR-DOMINI_IT.mailbox`, `IDT.mailbox`, `ITALIAN_POSTMAST.mailbox`) |
| [`guide-feedback/HYPERTXT.mailbox`](guide-feedback/HYPERTXT.mailbox) | feedback and exchanges about the guides and documentation |
| [`nerd-humor/JOKES.mailbox`](nerd-humor/JOKES.mailbox) | humour and jokes circulated among colleagues |
| [`marco-signatute.txt`](marco-signatute.txt) | email signatures used at the time |

The larger mailboxes keep the original headers (sender, date, SMTP path), useful
for reconstructing the mail flows of the time.

## Notes

- The `.mailbox` files are mail archives in `mbox` format; they open with a mail
  client or with tools that read `mbox`.
- The contents have not been modified; they are part of the recovered historical
  material.

The exchanges in the `technical/` section were also used to reconstruct the
historical domain-registration procedures: see
[Domain registration](../domain-registration/README.en.md).
