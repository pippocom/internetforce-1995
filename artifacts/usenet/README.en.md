# Usenet — FAQs, guides, texts and public traces

> **Internet Force 1995–1996 Historical Archive**
> Historical reconstruction and technical documentation based on original Internet Force materials preserved by Marco Iannacone.
> Author and archive curator: Marco Iannacone · https://pippo.com
> License: [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE)

During the Internet Force years and the years immediately afterwards, Marco
Iannacone published and maintained FAQs, numerous guides and popular texts on
Usenet about the Internet, Linux and telematic culture. Part of this material
came from the **“Guida ad Internet e alla Realtà Virtuale”**, also published on
pippo.com.

This directory also preserves screenshots and references to historical material
that remains visible in the public Google Groups archive. These traces show both
contemporary technical activity and public use of `@internetforce.com` and
`@intf.com` addresses across newsgroups covering very different subjects.

This directory contains **only some copies**  recovered as a sample and is
**not a complete archive**. The preserved files are not necessarily the first
published versions: FAQs were updated over time, and some surviving copies are
reposts or later versions. The historical Usenet archives did not index or keep
everything published at the time, and many other documents and versions are
probably lost. The collection remains open: further recovered material may be
added in the future.

## Examples of material

Among the preserved texts, as examples and **not as an exhaustive list**:

- *Faccine telematiche* (telematic smileys)
- *Hackers*
- *Collegarsi ad Internet da DOS* (getting online from DOS)
- *Linux FAQ*
- public traces of Internet Force accounts in 1996 newsgroups
- a technical post dated 22 March 1996 in which Marco Iannacone identifies
  himself as Internet Force's System Administrator and discusses a possible
  e-mail/GSM-SMS gateway
