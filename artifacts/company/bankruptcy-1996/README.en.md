# Bankruptcy of Internet Force S.r.l. — 31 October 1996

This document is the bankruptcy trustee's notice concerning **Internet Force S.r.l.**

The letter states that the **Court of Crema**, by a judgment filed on **31 October 1996**, declared the company bankrupt. It also identifies the delegated judge and bankruptcy trustee and explains the procedure for creditors to submit claims.

## Historical significance

The document provides a firm date for the legal outcome of the company:

**31 October 1996 — declaration of bankruptcy of Internet Force S.r.l.**

This date should not be confused with a single technical shutdown of the services. Material preserved in the repository shows that migration and infrastructure-transition work was already under way before the bankruptcy and continued around and beyond that date.

In 1996 the shareholders decided not to continue the company's activities. Internet Force S.r.l. was subsequently declared bankrupt by the Court of Crema, with the judgment filed on 31 October 1996.

The network and the associated technical work did not disappear at that moment: services were migrated, some provider/POP infrastructures were made autonomous, and Marco Iannacone subsequently continued separate, customized technical consulting work for several organizations.

## Related chronology

- **September–October 1996** — documented migration of services and DNS toward Enter;
- **22 October 1996** — documented technical acceptance of the Pesaro Point;
- **31 October 1996** — declaration of bankruptcy of Internet Force S.r.l.;
- **late 1996 and 1997** — further technical documentation relating to autonomous infrastructures and subsequent consulting work.

The document therefore makes it possible to distinguish the company's **legal end** from the progressive **migration and technical continuity of its infrastructure**.
