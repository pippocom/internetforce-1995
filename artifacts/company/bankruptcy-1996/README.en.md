# Closure and bankruptcy of Internet Force S.r.l. — 1996

This folder preserves company documentation relating to the final phase of
**Internet Force S.r.l.**

## Shareholders' meeting minutes — 24 September 1996

[`verbale_ultima_assemblea_INTF.pdf`](verbale_ultima_assemblea_INTF.pdf) is the
record of the ordinary shareholders' meeting held on **24 September 1996** at
the operating office in Via Plana 20, Milan.

The copy was supplied in 2026 by **Pascal Schait**, a founding shareholder and
Technical Director of Internet Force. The names of the participants and some
other information are redacted in the supplied copy. The document is preserved
**exactly as received**, without attempting to reconstruct or infer the
redacted names.

The agenda included approval of the financial position as of 12 September 1996
and a choice between **recapitalisation/loss coverage** and **liquidation**.

The minutes directly document the context in which the shareholders reached the
decision to stop the company's activity: rapid growth in the number of Internet
providers, severe price pressure, Telecom's entry into the Internet market,
commercial difficulties including in the professional-customer segment,
financial pressure, and the inability to continue without new capital.

The discussion considers alternatives including a drastic reduction of
operations and the sale of equipment, but records that none of the shareholders
was prepared to proceed with immediate recapitalisation. The chairman concludes
that, given the level of liabilities and the lack of room for manoeuvre, the
remaining viable route was a **voluntary bankruptcy filing**.

The minutes also document a competitor willing to take over the remaining
customer contracts without paying consideration. The contracts were annual and
prepaid; the purpose was to let customers reach the end of their paid term
without further charges while giving the competitor an opportunity to obtain
their renewals.

## Bankruptcy notice — 31 October 1996

[`bankruptcy-notice-1996.pdf`](bankruptcy-notice-1996.pdf) is the bankruptcy
trustee's notice concerning **Internet Force S.r.l.**

The letter states that the **Court of Crema**, by a judgment filed on
**31 October 1996**, declared the company bankrupt. It also identifies the
delegated judge and bankruptcy trustee and explains the procedure for creditors
to submit claims.

## Historical significance

Together the two documents now make it possible to distinguish two moments more
precisely:

- **24 September 1996** — the shareholders formally examine recapitalisation,
  liquidation and alternative solutions, concluding that the company cannot
  continue without new capital and moving toward a voluntary bankruptcy filing;
- **31 October 1996** — the Court of Crema formally declares Internet Force
  S.r.l. bankrupt.

These dates should not be confused with a single technical shutdown of the
services. Material preserved in the repository shows that migration and
infrastructure-transition work was already under way before the bankruptcy and
continued around and beyond that date.

The network and associated technical work did not disappear at the same moment
as the company: services were migrated, some provider/POP infrastructures were
made autonomous, and Marco Iannacone subsequently continued separate,
customized technical consulting work for several organizations.

## Related chronology

- **24 September 1996** — ordinary shareholders' meeting on the financial
  position, recapitalisation and closure;
- **September–October 1996** — documented migration of services and DNS toward
  Enter;
- **22 October 1996** — documented technical acceptance of the Pesaro Point;
- **31 October 1996** — declaration of bankruptcy of Internet Force S.r.l.;
- **late 1996 and 1997** — further technical documentation relating to
  autonomous infrastructures and subsequent consulting work.

The documents therefore distinguish the **company decision**, the company's
**legal end**, and the progressive **migration and technical continuity of its
infrastructure**.
