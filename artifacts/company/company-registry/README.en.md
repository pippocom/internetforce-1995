# Business Register — public Internet Force S.r.l. record

The screenshot preserved in this directory comes from the public Italian
Business Register search and shows **Internet Force S.r.l.** as a Milan local
unit.

## Visible information

The record shows:

- **Internet Force S.r.l.**
- **Milan local unit**
- address: **Via Plana Giovanni Antonio 20, Milan**
- generic legal form: **capital company**
- status: **ceased/deleted**
- an activity description that includes, among other items,
  **data transmission and reception** and data storage on magnetic and/or
  optical media.

The address matches the **Business Price List dated 15 June 1996**, where
Internet Force prints **“Sede Operativa: Via Plana, 20, Milano”**.

The consulting agreement dated 2 January 1996 instead gives the company's
registered office in **Pandino (CR)**. The two records are not inconsistent:
the documentation distinguishes the legal/registered office from the operating
Milan local unit.

## Note on the ATECO classification

The current screenshot displays code **73.30.0 — Public relations and
communication**. This is not used to reconstruct the administrative
classification assigned to the company in 1995–1996.

The **ATECO '91** classification did not contain a specific entry for
“Internet access provision (Provider)”. In ISTAT's official correspondence table
between ATECO 2002 and ATECO '91, **64.20.5 — Fornitura di accesso a Internet
(Provider)** in ATECO 2002 maps back to the broader **64.20.0 —
Telecommunications** category in ATECO '91.

ISTAT source:

<https://www.istat.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Tavola-di-raccordo-ATECO-2002-ATECO-91.pdf>
