# Consulting and Assistance Agreement — 2 January 1996

This document is the consulting and assistance agreement signed by **Internet Force S.r.l.** and **Marco Iannacone** on **2 January 1996**.

The date of the agreement **does not mark the beginning of the collaboration**. The professional relationship had already been in place throughout 1995, including the period in which Internet Force was being built and launched. During 1995, Iannacone worked continuously for the company and invoiced his services monthly; the January 1996 agreement formalized in writing a relationship that already existed.

## Why this document matters

The agreement formally defines Iannacone's role as **Senior System and Network Administrator** and provides a contemporary description of the technical scope of the position.

The required skills include:

- Unix system administration;
- TCP/IP and internetworking;
- DNS;
- routing;
- RAID;
- firewalls.

The operational responsibilities also include:

- day-to-day operation and maintenance of Internet Force hardware and software;
- periodic system backups;
- administration of a heterogeneous network including Windows PCs, Macintosh computers and Unix systems;
- administration of Unix environments including **SunOS, FreeBSD and Linux**.

The document allows Iannacone's operational role and the skills involved in building and running the Internet Force infrastructure to be reconstructed.

## Timeline

- **1995** — collaboration already under way during the construction and launch of the provider;
- **2 January 1996** — the existing consulting relationship is formalized in writing.
