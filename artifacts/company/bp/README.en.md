This folder preserves contemporary documentation from the design and startup
phase of Internet Force.

## Net Way / Steve Maoz presentation — 21 September 1995

[`INTF1995_NW_Steve_Maoz_Presentation.pdf`](INTF1995_NW_Steve_Maoz_Presentation.pdf)
is a presentation prepared by **Steve Maoz / Net Way**, dated
**21 September 1995**.

The copy preserved in this repository was supplied in 2026 by
**Pascal Schait**, a founding shareholder and Technical Director of Internet Force.
Some portions are redacted in the supplied copy. The PDF is preserved
**exactly as received**, with no attempt to reconstruct the redacted material.

Among other things, the document records:

- an initial organization chart with **two Technical Directors** and
  Marco Iannacone listed separately as **System Administrator**;
- a proposed future model in which operations are divided into
  **Office Computer/LAN, System, Security, Connectivity & Networking** functions;
- a representation of technical contributors, with **Xpert** associated with
  the Operating System, **IDT** with Internet Connectivity, and **Net Way**
  coordinating/composing the project;
- an **installation schedule** placing UNIX hardware delivery, operating-system
  installation, software installation and training within the same startup plan.

The schedule is treated as evidence of the **plan as of September 1995**, not
as automatic proof that every activity was completed on the exact listed date.

### Integration with first-person testimony

The presentation is interpreted together with Marco Iannacone's direct
first-person testimony. In particular:

- the two initial Technical Directors were **Pascal Schait and Maurizio Gerosa**;
- Gerosa later stepped down from the Technical Director role while remaining a
  shareholder and retaining responsibility for the office LAN computer fleet;
- Marco's initial role was **System & Network Administrator**, with an
  expectation that, as the ISP grew, LAN, systems, security and
  connectivity/networking would progressively gain more junior specialist staff;
- Steve Maoz, IDT's commercial contact through Net Way, brought
  **Xpert UNIX Systems** into the project as technical prime contractor,
  building on previous work with Xpert on ISP deployments in Israel and North Africa.

These details are identified as **first-person testimony** where the PDF itself
does not state them explicitly.
