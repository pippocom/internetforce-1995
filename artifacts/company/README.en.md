# Company and commercial material

> **Internet Force 1995–1996 Historical Archive**
> Historical reconstruction and technical documentation based on original Internet Force materials preserved by Marco Iannacone.
> Author and archive curator: Marco Iannacone · https://pippo.com
> License: [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE)

This area collects the surviving company and commercial documentation of
Internet Force together with a public Business Register record useful for
cross-checking company and operational details.

## Areas

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`bp/`](bp/README.en.md) | **Net Way / Steve Maoz presentation dated 21 September 1995**: initial organization chart, proposed future model, Net Way/Xpert/IDT technical contributions and installation schedule; contemporary copy supplied in 2026 by Pascal Schait |
| [`business-services-price-list-1996/`](business-services-price-list-1996/README.en.md) | **Business Price List dated 15 June 1996**: business connectivity, static IP and LAN services, leased lines, domains, DNS, Web-page production and 1–10 MB hosting; document preserved and supplied by Pascal Schait, a founding shareholder of Internet Force |
| [`bankruptcy-1996/`](bankruptcy-1996/README.en.md) | company documentation concerning the **decision to close and the 1996 bankruptcy**: the 24 September shareholders' meeting minutes, the trustee's notice and the judgment of the Court of Crema |
| [`marco-iannacone-consulting-contract-1996/`](marco-iannacone-consulting-contract-1996/README.en.md) | documentation of **Marco Iannacone's consulting engagement** with Internet Force: the January 1996 contract and the Senior System and Network Administrator role |
| [`company-registry/`](company-registry/README.en.md) | screenshot of the public **Business Register** record: Milan local unit at Via Plana 20, ceased/deleted status and activity description |
