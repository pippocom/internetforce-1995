# Press coverage and press releases

> **Internet Force 1995–1996 Historical Archive**
> Historical reconstruction and technical documentation based on original Internet Force materials preserved by Marco Iannacone.
> Author and archive curator: Marco Iannacone · https://pippo.com

This folder collects **contemporary press material** relating to Internet Force.
It includes both **external press coverage**, useful as independent evidence of
services and market presence, and **company press releases**, documenting what
Internet Force publicly stated while it was operating.

Copyright in third-party articles, layout and images remains with the respective
rights holders; the scans are kept as non-commercial historical documentation.
Company press releases are preserved as contemporary primary sources.

## Internet Force press releases

[`Comunicati_stamp_internetforce.pdf`](Comunicati_stamp_internetforce.pdf)
collects contemporary Internet Force press releases. The copy was supplied in
2026 by **Pascal Schait**, a founding shareholder and Technical Director of the
company. Some portions are redacted in the supplied copy; the PDF is preserved
without attempting to reconstruct the censored text.

The releases document, among other things, Web customers and online presences of
the period, including *Cosmopolitan*, Edizioni Motori (*Auto Capital* and
*Autopiù*), *Storia del XX Secolo*, Kraft, Designer Exchange, Canale Moda,
Clear/Unilever and Motta.

Marco Iannacone's direct testimony adds the operational detail: for these
customers Internet Force provided both **Web development and hosting**. The
sites were predominantly static/presentation sites; newspaper and magazine
editorial teams regularly supplied or updated pages and editorial content. The
workflow was manual, without a CMS: authoring, checking and publication were
distinct activities. Marco also developed small CGI programs and, after PHP/FI
was introduced in 1996, small PHP applications for specific needs.
