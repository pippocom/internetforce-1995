# Internet Force in *Internet & Musica*

This directory preserves a scan of an article published by **Internet & Musica**, written by **Andrea Maffini** for the magazine's *Prova il provider* section and devoted to **Internet Force**.

The article is a particularly useful contemporary source because it describes Internet Force not only as an Internet access provider, but also as a company offering services, consulting and solutions for businesses and professionals.

## Historical points of interest

The article documents, among other things:

- the Internet Force partner / POP network;
- the company's focus on businesses and professionals;
- the use of **Check Point Firewall-1**;
- mail services referring to **POP3**, **MIME** and **IMAP**;
- Web hosting and publication on Internet Force servers;
- screenshots of the Internet Force website and hosted sites;
- operational references to POP locations and contact details.

At the top right of the second page there is also a screenshot of a **VRML page** produced as part of Internet Force's Web experimentation. This is historically notable as evidence of very early experimentation with 3D Web technology in Italy, while VRML was still an emerging technology. The technical context here in the repository is in the [Web, FTP, news and mailing lists](../../../docs/05-web-news-ftp.en.md) chapter.
