
Hardware:

HUB: 3C16670 LinkBuilder FMS II
Tranceiver: Etherlink Tranceiver
            3C583A on CISCO AUI

Connections:
             The tranceiver connect a port of a HUB to
             the router.


--------------------- Buffering Log --------------------

The following instructions will enable the buffering log in the CISCO:

logging buffered
service timestamps debug datetime
service timestamps log datetime

than do

clock set

and fix the correct time.

To view the log it is enough to enter:

show log
