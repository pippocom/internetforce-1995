# Wayback Machine — post-Internet-Force Web traces

This directory preserves two screenshots of 1997 Wayback Machine snapshots.
Both post-date Internet Force's operating period and document continuity of
domains and organisations that emerged or changed during the wind-down.

## `internetforce.com` — 5 June 1997

Snapshot:

<https://web.archive.org/web/19970605025818/http://internetforce.com/>

The earliest available Wayback capture of `internetforce.com` is dated
**5 June 1997**, after the central-services migration had already taken place.
The page shows the website of **Enter**, the provider to which the remaining
Milan customers and central services were migrated.

The historical value of the capture is therefore the post-migration continuity:
the `internetforce.com` domain is already serving Enter's site.

File: `internetforce-com-1997-06-05.jpeg`

## `pesaro.com` — 3 May 1997

Snapshot:

<https://web.archive.org/web/19970503204351/http://www.pesaro.com/>

The **3 May 1997** capture shows **Pesaro Point** already operating as an
independent organisation. The page nevertheless retains the **Internet Force
I.S.P.** mark next to the Pesaro.com/Pesaro Point logo, advertises Internet
connectivity services and includes a **“Guida ad Internet” curated by Marco
Iannacone**.

The snapshot therefore documents visible continuity between the former Pesaro
POP and the later independent activity.

File: `pesaro-com-1997-05-03.jpeg`
